Order primers from PAN by logging in and clicking Oligo Synthesis.

http://pan.stanford.edu/section_html/OLIGOSYN/index.html

Information needed to order primers:

1. PTA#

2. Number of Oligos, Scale (25 nmol, 40nmol, 200 nmol, 1.0 umol), purified yes/no, primer sequences and any modifications.

3. Authorization from Molly Schumer to use PTA


The turn around time is extremely fast so you will not get an email when the primers are synthesized, you must check your order history for the status of the primers.
