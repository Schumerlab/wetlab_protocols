After each sequencing run, we wash the flow cell and add storage buffer according to ONT's protocol, which can be found on Dropbox:

The Altemose Lab has found that the following protocol modification helps with pore recovery: 
***Instead of just injecting the wash and letting it sit, we aspirated up and down lightly at each injection.***
